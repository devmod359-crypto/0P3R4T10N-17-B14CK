    Call bets

    Each button places one chip of your current chip value on every part of the bet. The cost is the chip value times the number of chips.

    Bet types and payouts
    Straight up
    One number. Pays 35 to 1.
    Split
    Two adjacent numbers, including 0 with 1, 2 or 3. Pays 17 to 1.
    Street
    Three numbers in a row. Pays 11 to 1.
    Trio
    0-1-2 or 0-2-3. Pays 11 to 1.
    Corner
    Four numbers that meet at a point. Pays 8 to 1.
    First four
    0-1-2-3. Pays 8 to 1.
    Six line
    Two neighboring streets. Pays 5 to 1.
    Dozen or column
    Twelve numbers. Pays 2 to 1.
    Red or black, odd or even, 1–18 or 19–36
    Eighteen numbers. Pays 1 to 1. Zero loses all of these.
